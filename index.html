The Studio

One person.
Many unsent drafts.

Dandelion Wish is an independent studio built around a single question: what happens in the space between what we feel and what we actually say?

We build games that sit in that gap — intimate, carefully crafted experiences that treat emotional realism as a design pillar, not an afterthought.

We ship quietly. We let things be discovered. We believe the right game finds the right person.

01

Emotional realism

Not fantasy power. Not escapism. The specific texture of real, complicated feelings — rendered playable.

02

Discovery over marketing

We don't shout. We leave breadcrumbs. The games find their audience on their own terms.

03

The medium as message

Form and content are inseparable. Every UI choice, every sound, every pixel carries weight.
